Colour picker · how it is now

Two circles, then eight more

The picker sits under CONFIRM. Default is a circle split into the eight palette colours. Tapping it opens all eight underneath, which wrap onto two rows and push START MATCH further down. Custom opens the phone's own colour wheel.

Once you've picked, the phone's highlight colour changes to yours. The TV already sends every player's colour to every phone, but the picker doesn't show which colours other players have.

Colour picker · three ways to rehaul it

A · Palette on screen

All eight colours are always showing, in a 3 × 3 grid with a + tile for the colour wheel. Colours other players have are faded and marked with their initial. One tap to change, and no Default or Custom step. It takes up more of the screen, all the time.

B · Your stone is the picker

The screen shows your picked stone in your colour. Tapping it slides up a panel with the same grid. The choose screen stays short, and you see the colour on the thing it applies to. Changing colour takes one extra tap.

C · Pick your colour when you join

You pick your colour next to your name, on the join screen. The choose screen is then just stones and START MATCH. You can change it later from the "Mira · change colour" link. The join screen gets busier, and it's the very first thing a new player sees.
